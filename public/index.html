Why this matters

India runs one of the largest welfare systems on earth — yet crores of eligible citizens never receive their benefit. The barrier is rarely money. It is information: dozens of portals, English-only interfaces, forms nobody explains, and middlemen who profit from that confusion.

  • Discovery — one profile, every scheme you qualify for, with the reason written out.
  • Language — the whole interface adapts to 12 Indian languages, with voice in and out.
  • Paperwork — a readiness score tells you exactly which document is missing and where to get it.
  • Rights — an RTI or grievance letter you can print and submit today.
  • Trust — a fraud shield that reads a suspicious message and tells you what to do.

Start in 30 seconds

  1. Tap Scheme Finder.
  2. Fill only what you know — leave the rest blank.
  3. Press Check eligibility. Read the reasons, not just the list.
  4. Open Document Checker to see what to collect.
  5. Use RTI & Letters if the office stalls.
  6. Keep Helplines saved, and check any doubtful SMS in Fraud Shield.

Built for real constraints

🧓 Designed for first-time internet users

Large text (A± up to 140%), high-contrast mode, plain-language labels, and every answer can be read aloud. No jargon without a definition.

📴 Works where the network doesn't

Installs as a PWA and caches the whole app. The eligibility engine, document guide, letter templates and fraud rules all run on the device — no internet needed.

🔗 Nothing invented, everything sourced

Every scheme card links to its official .gov.in portal. The AI is grounded on a curated knowledge base and told never to promise what it cannot cite.

    How to use Bharat Setu — step by step
    
    
    
      
    1. Find schemes
    Go to Scheme Finder, fill your age, income, area and tick your situation. Press Check eligibility. You get a ranked list — green cards are strong matches. Open “Why you qualify” to see the exact rule that matched.
    
      
    2. Get your papers ready
    Open Document Checker and tick what you already have. The readiness score shows your gaps, and each missing document tells you which office issues it and how long it usually takes.
    
      
    3. Claim, and escalate if needed
    Apply on the official portal in each card. If the office delays or refuses, use RTI & Letters to generate a formal letter — an RTI reply is legally due in 30 days.
